Privacy policy

Shelfie · Updated 22 November 2026

Shelfie keeps only what it needs to run your shelf and routine. No ads, no tracking, and we never sell your data.

Who we are

Shelfie is made by Studio Tubi, an independent software studio in the Netherlands. Studio Tubi is responsible for the personal data described here. Questions or requests: support@studiotubi.com.

What we keep

  • Your account. You sign in with Apple. Apple gives us an account ID, your email address (or a private relay address if you choose to hide it) and, the first time, your name.
  • What you add. Your products and their details (name, brand, type, size, barcode, notes, opened and expiry dates), your routine and its schedule, your skin type and skin goals, your reminder times, and your hydration log (daily goal and glasses per day).
  • Sign-in sessions. Tokens that keep you signed in, so you don't have to sign in on every open.
  • Technical data. Our server records requests (such as the time, the page of the app it came from and how long it took) to keep Shelfie running and fix problems. This is kept for a short time only.

What stays on your phone

The camera is only used to read barcodes, and images never leave your phone. Reminders are scheduled on your phone by iOS. Shelfie has no analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

Who else sees it

  • Hosting. Your data is stored on servers of Scaleway in Amsterdam, the Netherlands.
  • Monitoring. We use Datadog to watch the server's health and performance. It receives the technical data above, not the contents of your shelf.
  • Barcode lookups. When you scan a barcode, Shelfie looks the number up in our own product list and, if needed, in Open Beauty Facts and UPCitemdb. Only the barcode number is sent; these services can also see your IP address, as any website can.
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple under its own privacy policy.

We don't share your data with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

Why we use it

We use your data to provide the app you asked for: your account, shelf, routine, reminders and hydration log (performance of a contract). Technical data is used to keep the service secure and working (our legitimate interest).

How long we keep it

Your data is kept for as long as you have an account. You can delete your account at any time in the app under You → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, products, routine and hydration log from our server right away.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, or object to how we use it, by emailing support@studiotubi.com. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Children

Shelfie is not meant for children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
