Privacy policy

Shelfie · Updated 22 November 2026

Shelfie keeps only what it needs to run your shelf and routine. No ads, no tracking, and we never sell your data.

Who we are

Shelfie is made by Studio Tubi, an independent software studio in the Netherlands.

Who else sees it

Your data is stored with Scaleway in Amsterdam. We use Datadog to monitor the server, and barcode numbers you scan may be looked up in Open Beauty Facts and UPCitemdb. Apple handles Sign in with Apple. We don't share your data with anyone else.

Deleting your data

Delete your account any time in the app under You → Delete account. This removes all your data from our server right away.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can access, correct or delete your data. You can also complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
