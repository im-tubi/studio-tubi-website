Privacy policy

Shelfie · Updated 22 November 2026

Shelfie keeps only what it needs to run your shelf and routine. No ads, no tracking, and we never sell your data.

Who we are

Shelfie is made by Studio Tubi, an independent software studio in the Netherlands.

What we keep

Your account details from Sign in with Apple and what you add to the app, such as your products, routine and hydration log. We use it only to run Shelfie for you.

Who handles it

We use trusted service providers for hosting and keeping the app running. They only process your data on our behalf and protect it to the same standard.

Deleting your data

Delete your account any time in the app under You → Delete account. This removes all your data right away.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can access, correct or delete your data. You can also complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
